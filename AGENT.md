# Agent Instructions

Understand the business before making changes.

Pezeshk Afzar is a healthcare technology company — but the website is not about
hospitals, machines, or clinical procedure. It is about **living**. The product
of good healthcare is an ordinary, joyful day: a walk outside, a kite on a hill,
dinner with people you love. Every design and copy decision should point at that
outcome, not at the machinery that enables it.

Reference experience: **getmaple.ca** — a health platform that feels warm,
human, and alive rather than clinical.

## Design Principles

* Life and vitality first: the UI should evoke breath, warmth, and joy.
* Warm, organic, approachable — never cold, sterile, or corporate.
* Nature-derived palette (warm ivory, forest green, maple coral, mint).
* Soft, generous geometry: pills, arches, organic blobs, large radii.
* Trust earned through warmth and clarity, not through gravity and jargon.

## UX Rules

* Avoid e-commerce patterns, shopping-cart behavior, and product-store layouts.
* Avoid hospital-administration aesthetics: no cold dashboards, no sterile grids.
* Prioritize conversation and connection ("say hello", "let's talk") over
  transactional CTAs ("submit", "request demo").
* Interactions should feel gentle and alive: breathing shapes, soft reveals,
  friendly hovers. Motion supports the feeling of life; it never shouts.

## Copy Voice

* Warm, human-centric, and uplifting. Write to a person, not to a procurement
  department.
* Talk about outcomes in life terms (good days, time back, feeling like
  yourself) before technology terms.
* Plain verbs, sentence case, no jargon walls. Technical credibility appears in
  supporting detail, never in the headline.
* Both English and Persian copy carry the same warmth; Persian is written
  natively, never translated word-for-word.

## Software Products

Present software as the quiet helper behind good care: what it takes off
people's plates, the time it gives back, the calm it creates. Capabilities,
reliability, and security may be discussed as supporting reassurance, while
proprietary implementation details, internal architectures, and sensitive
technical information remain confidential.

## Medical Technologies

Present technologies as bringers of good days — innovations chosen because they
help people feel like themselves again. Emphasize human impact first, clinical
importance second, and never disclose confidential implementation details.

## Imagery

* People enjoying life: laughing outdoors, flying kites, running at sunrise,
  gardening, family moments.
* Never: medical equipment close-ups, hospital rooms, doctors posing with
  tablets, stock-corporate handshakes.

## Development Rules

* Read related files first.
* Modify only necessary files.
* Preserve translations and i18n keys (locales: en, fa only; fa is RTL).
* Preserve responsive behavior and flawless RTL support.
* Keep components reusable.
* Avoid unnecessary dependencies.

Before major changes, explain the implementation plan.
